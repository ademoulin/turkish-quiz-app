# Grammar audit: second implementation pass

## Changes

- Lessons 1 and 2 offer meaning and typed production. Simple action-chunk/main-predicate/whole-sentence questions are removed from scored practice and random/review pools; existing bracketed worked examples retain the introductory boundary explanation. Mixed sessions use six questions from each remaining task, with different model sentences.
- Lesson 4 now offers **Choose the clause’s case form**. The blank sentence includes its genitive actor and supplied main predicate; three complete forms keep action and agreement constant and vary outer case. For example, `Bizim yardım ___ sevindi.` offers `etmemizi`, `etmemize`, `etmemizden`. There is no English translation or required-case cue. The learner must connect the case to the supplied predicate.
- Typed drills retain their explicitly requested case and strict Turkish grading. Lesson 2B remains the activity-form selection lesson rather than duplicating it in Lesson 2.
