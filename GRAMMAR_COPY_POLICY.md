## Exceptions retained deliberately

- 2B wanting questions request the common bare-infinitive pattern. Activity-subject questions request an infinitive. Other forms can be grammatical in broader usage.
- 12B/C nominalizer requests are retained, especially where -mA and -DIK can overlap with evaluative predicates. They do not prescribe the outer case that 12C asks the learner to choose.
- 11H and 12A retain the requested English timing construction because grammatical paraphrases can overlap. The question no longer supplies its suffix formula or repeats the event-order solution.
- Supplied -ken linking bases remain, keeping finite conjugation outside Grammar.
- Overt -ları/-leri agreement for onlar remains explicit wherever exact typed grading requires it.
